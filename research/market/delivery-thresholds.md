### Intra-City Delivery Options from Vyttila Hub:
1. **Local Bike Runner / In-House Delivery (0–7 km radius):**
   - Covers: Vyttila, Kadavanthra, Panampilly Nagar, Ravipuram, MG Road, Palarivattom, Kakkanad (Infopark), Maradu, Tripunithura, Thrikkakara.

   - Base fare for 0–4 km: **₹40.00 – ₹55.00**.

3. **Local Courier (ST Courier, Professional Couriers, DTDC Ernakulam):**

## 3. Recommended Delivery Policy for Stationery Point

### Recommendation: "Free Delivery Across Kochi on Orders Over ₹499"

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ORDERS OVER ₹499 :  FREE Doorstep Delivery across Greater Kochi            │
│  ORDERS UNDER ₹499:  Flat ₹40 Delivery Fee (or Free In-Store Pickup)        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Financial & Margin Defensibility Working:

1. **Gross Margin on Stationery Basket:**
   - Standard stationery retail gross margin: **28% to 35%**.
   - On a minimum qualifying order of **₹499.00**, gross profit generated is:
     $$\text{Gross Profit} = ₹499 \times 30\% = \mathbf{₹149.70}$$
2. **Delivery Cost Absorption:**
   - Local delivery cost (courier / bike runner): **₹40.00**.
   - Net contribution profit remaining after delivery absorption:
     $$\text{Net Contribution} = ₹149.70 - ₹40.00 = \mathbf{₹109.70}$$
3. **Psychological & Competitive Alignment:**
   - **₹499** perfectly aligns with the two dominant benchmarks known to every Kerala shopper: Amazon India (**₹499**) and DC Books (**₹500**).
   - Setting the threshold higher (e.g. ₹999) discourages parents looking to buy standard 4-notebook bundles or mid-term top-ups.
   - Setting the threshold lower (e.g. ₹249) erodes margin on low-ticket items like a single pencil box.
   - All recommended pre-made kit price bands (Band 1: ₹499, Band 2: ₹999, Band 3: ₹1,999, Band 4: ₹2,999) automatically qualify for Free Delivery, eliminating checkout hesitation on the core bundles.
