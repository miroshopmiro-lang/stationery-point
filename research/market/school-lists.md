## Summary of Patterns for Kochi Stationery Procurement

1. **Notebook Formats:**
   - Primary (KG–Grades 1-2): 4-line 100/200pg (English/EVS), Medium/Small Square 100/200pg (Maths), 2-line 100/200pg (Malayalam/Hindi), Unruled 100pg (Journal), Classmate 40pg Drawing book, Classmate 28-30pg Scrapbook.
   - Middle (Grades 3–8): Single-line 200pg standard, Horizontal Single-line 200pg with working column (Maths specific), Interleaf 100/200pg (Science/AI/Lab).
   - High School (Grades 9–12): Single-line 200pg (College/A4 size), Graph Book, Outline Maps (10 India Political, 5 World Political), Lab Practical Record Books (Hardbound interleaf).
2. **Standard Brand Mentions:**
   - Classmate (ITC) explicitly specified for Drawing and Scrap Books.
   - SCERT / Kerala State Board Padavali textbooks standard across all CBSE schools in Kerala for compulsory Malayalam language.
