## 2. Popular Return-Gift Item Categories in Kerala

1. **Themed Mini Stationery Kits (₹30–₹70/unit):**
   - Multi-piece sets containing 2 pencils, 1 ruler, 1 shaped eraser, 1 sharpener, and 1 mini memo pad in character pouches (Space, Dinosaur, Unicorn, Superhero).
2. **DIY Craft & Creative Kits (₹80–₹200/unit):**
   - Clay modelling dough sets, DIY sun-catcher glass painting, origami sets, wooden puzzle cutouts, sand art cards.
3. **Activity Books & Spot UV Colouring Books (₹100–₹280/unit):**
   - High-GSM colouring books, mandala drawing pads, sticker activity books.
4. **Utility Tabletop & Desk Items (₹150–₹350/unit):**
   - Wooden pen stands with memo clips, magnetic to-do lists, miniature decorative trees, handcrafted hardbound pocket diaries.
5. **Eco-Friendly & Seed Stationery (₹40–₹90/unit):**
   - Plantable seed pencils and seed paper notebooks (growing popularity in Ernakulam schools with "Haritha / Green Rules" initiatives).

## 4. Key Takeaways for Stationery Point Website Strategy

1. **Pre-Packed Bundles of 10 / 20 / 30 Units:**
   - Buyers want fixed-count multi-packs rather than adding single items 30 times. Display "Class Pack (30 Pcs) - ₹1,199" or "Party Pack (15 Pcs) - ₹899" on WhatsApp enquiry cards.
2. **Age-Appropriate Curation:**
   - Tag return gifts clearly by age band: `Age 3–5 (Crayons & Dough)`, `Age 6–9 (Craft Kits & Puzzles)`, `Age 10+ (Diaries & Pen Sets)`.
3. **Turnaround Time Expectations:**
   - Kerala event buyers typically order 3 to 7 days before the birthday/event date. WhatsApp order flow must confirm immediate stock readiness.
