## 2. Strategic Stocking & Merchandising Strategy for Stationery Point

1. **Everyday Core Traffic Drivers (Stock 100% Depth):**
   - **Camlin, Apsara, Doms, Faber-Castell, Kangaro, Luxor.**
   - These 6 brands constitute 85%+ of walk-in and WhatsApp basket volume. Must always be in stock at standard MRP with pre-bundled kits.
2. **High-Margin Gifting & Milestone Anchor:**
   - **Parker.**
   - Dedicate a prominent display showcase for Parker Vector/Jotter/IM gift boxes near the billing counter for instant impulse purchase.
3. **Art & Creative Differentiation (Moat Against General Corner Shops):**
   - **Brustro & Staedtler.**
   - Stocking Brustro sketchbooks, gouache, and Staedtler graphite pencils captures the high-value fine arts student market (RLV College Tripunithura and Kochi design studios) who currently have to order online or travel to Broadway.
4. **On-Demand / Niche Procurement:**
   - **Winsor & Newton, Rotring.**
   - Maintain selective basic SKUs (Rotring Tikky 0.5/0.7mm, Winsor & Newton Cotman 12-half pan set) and offer 24-hour WhatsApp special ordering for technical and professional artists.
