# B2B Procurement Standards: How Indian Schools & Offices Buy Stationery in Bulk

**Date Accessed:** 17 August 2026  
**Methodology:** Direct synthesis of standard Indian procurement rules (GeM General Terms & Conditions, General Financial Rules / GFR 2017 Rule 149, CBIC GST HSN Classification Guidelines, and private school/office purchasing conventions in Kerala).

---

## 1. Normal Payment Terms & Credit Periods

| Buyer Category | Standard Payment Terms | Standard Credit Period | Payment Milestone Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Government & Public Institutions (via GeM)** | 100% online payment post-acceptance | **10 days** from generation of Consignee Receipt and Acceptance Certificate (CRAC) | CRAC is issued digitally within 48–72 hours after physical receipt and inspection. Under GFR Rule 149, payments must be cleared within 10 days of CRAC. |
| **Private CBSE / ICSE Schools (Annual Stock)** | 30% Advance + 70% Net 30 | **30 to 45 days** post complete delivery | Schools require delivery of bulk textbooks/notebooks 2–3 weeks before academic reopening (April/May), with final settlement after verification of counted cartons. |
| **Corporate Offices & IT Parks (e.g., Infopark Kochi)** | 100% against Monthly Consolidated Invoice | **Net 30 days** | Monthly delivery against approved Purchase Order (PO); vendor submits single monthly invoice with signed delivery challans (DC) attached. |
| **Small Businesses / Clinics / Local Firms** | Immediate UPI / Bank Transfer or 7-Day Net | **0 to 7 days** | Often buy via WhatsApp with doorstep cash-on-delivery (COD) or instant UPI upon physical delivery. |

## 2. GST Invoicing Expectations & HSN Classification

### A. Critical Stationery HSN Codes & GST Rates

Under Indian Goods and Services Tax (GST) laws administered by CBIC:

| HSN Code | Description / Commodity | Applicable GST Rate (%) | Input Tax Credit (ITC) Eligibility for B2B Buyers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HSN 4820** | Exercise books, registers, notebooks, order books, receipt books, blotting pads | **12%** | Yes (eligible for commercial offices and taxable entities) |
| **HSN 9608** | Ballpoint pens, gel pens, fountain pens, marker pens, refill cartridges | **18%** | Yes |
| **HSN 9609** | Pencils (lead/graphite), crayons, pencil leads, pastels, drawing charcoals | **12%** | Yes |
| **HSN 9017** | Drawing, marking-out or mathematical calculating instruments (Geometry Boxes, Rulers) | **18%** | Yes |
| **HSN 4016 / 3926**| Rubber erasers, plastic clips, desktop organizers | **18%** | Yes |
| **HSN 3506** | Adhesives, liquid craft glue, glue sticks (Fevicol, PVA glue) | **18%** | Yes |
| **HSN 4202** | School bags, backpacks, instrument pouches, lunch bags | **18%** | Yes |
| **HSN 4802** | Uncoated A4 / A3 copier paper (JK Copier, Bilt, TNPL 70/75/80 GSM) | **12%** | Yes |
| **HSN 4901** | Printed textbooks, educational children's picture books | **0% (NIL)** | Exempt / No ITC |

### B. Invoicing & Documentation Requirements for Schools & Corporate Buyers
1. **GSTIN Verification:** Corporate offices and educational trusts claim ITC on office supplies and administrative stationery; they require a clear tax invoice displaying their registered legal entity name, address, and 15-digit GSTIN.
2. **E-Invoicing Compliance:** Under GST Notification No. 10/2023-Central Tax, B2B suppliers with aggregate annual turnover exceeding ₹5 Crore must issue IRN (Invoice Reference Number) generated e-invoices with signed QR codes.
3. **Delivery Challans (DC):** Deliveries must be accompanied by triplicate delivery challans signed and stamped by the receiving storekeeper/admin.

## 4. Quotation, Tender & RFQ Formats

1. **Formal Request for Quotation (RFQ):**
   - School administrative officers and office admins issue itemized RFQ tables specifying brand preferences (e.g., "Classmate only", "JK Copier 75 GSM", "Reynolds 045").
   - Suppliers must submit formal PDF quotes broken down into: `Unit Base Rate`, `Discount %`, `Taxable Value`, `GST % (CGST + SGST)`, and `Total Net Amount`.
2. **Purchase Order (PO):**
   - B2B transactions are formalized only upon receipt of an official signed PO specifying delivery deadlines and agreed payment terms.
3. **Sample Submission Protocol:**
   - For custom printed items (school diaries, student progress report covers, school notebooks, customized ID card lanyards), physical sample approval is mandatory prior to production run.

## 5. What Procurement Officers & Office Admins Expect from a Supplier

1. **Brand Reliability & Consistency:** Strict adherence to specified brands — substituting requested Classmate notebooks or Camlin geometry boxes with unbranded generic alternatives leads to immediate consignment rejection.
2. **Credit Flexibility (Net 30):** Institutions rarely pay cash on delivery for bulk orders; having pre-vetted credit lines or flexible 15–30 day settlement terms is the primary differentiator in winning B2B contracts.
3. **Doorstep Bulk Handling & Floor Delivery:** In Kochi, schools and offices expect carton delivery directly to their basement store or second-floor administrative wing, not curb drop-off.
4. **Hassle-Free Damaged Stock Exchange:** Immediate replacement of damaged cartons, defective pen packs, or misprinted pages within 24–48 hours without administrative friction.
5. **Single Consolidated Monthly Billing:** Admins prefer one itemized monthly bill rather than processing multiple small receipts.
