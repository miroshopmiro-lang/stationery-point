### Justification for These 4 Tiers:
1. **Band 1 (₹499 — "Essential Starter & Return Gift Set"):**
   - *Contents:* 12-shade Oil Pastels + 2 Drawing Books + 10 Pencils + Dust-Free Eraser + Sharpener + FeviStick + Zipper Pencil Pouch.
   - *Psychology:* ₹499 is the universal psychological threshold in Indian digital retail for impulsive gifting and starter kits. Fits directly under the ₹500 gifting budget.
2. **Band 2 (₹999 — "Complete Primary School Kit [Classes 1–4]"):**
   - *Contents:* 6 Four-Line + 2 Two-Line + 4 Square Ruled + 2 Drawing Books + Pencil Pack + Erasers + Blunt Scissor + Glue + 24 Wax Crayons + Book Covers & Labels.
   - *Psychology:* Solves 100% of a primary school child’s annual stationery checklist in one single WhatsApp message for under ₹1,000.
3. **Band 3 (₹1,499 — "Standard Middle School Academic Pack [Classes 5–8]"):**
   - *Contents:* 8 A4 Long Notebooks + 4 Science Interleaf + 2 Graph Books + Metal Geometry Box + 10 Gel/Ball Pens + Highlighters Pack + 30cm Scale + Poster Colour Set + Drawing Pad.
   - *Psychology:* Standard Back-to-School procurement for parents of middle schoolers. Substantial bulk discount compared to buying loose at school store.
4. **Band 4 (₹2,499 — "Deluxe Academic + Fine Art & Craft Studio Kit"):**
   - *Contents:* Complete Middle/High School Notebooks + Complete Pen & Instrument Set + Premium Brustro / Camlin Acrylic/Watercolour Set + 5 Synthetic Brushes + Heavyweight Canvas/Cartridge Pad + Desk Organiser + Geometry & Technical Drafting Set.
   - *Psychology:* Captures high-value shoppers, creative students, and parents seeking complete Back-to-School readiness in one package.

### Buyer Behavior & Operational Norms:
- **Order Timing:** Inquiries typically arrive 5 to 10 days before the event via WhatsApp.
- **Packaging Expectation:** Buyers expect individual transparent gift wrapping (shrink wrap or cellophane with ribbons) or pre-packed themed gift bags.
- **Sample Request:** Inquiring parents on WhatsApp routinely ask for photo samples of 2–3 options at their specific per-unit budget (e.g. *"Show me options for 35 kids at ₹100 each"*).

## 5. Bulk & Institutional Stationery Procurement in India (Schools, Colleges & Corporate Offices)

Commercial procurement for educational institutions and corporate offices in Kochi (Infopark Kakkanad, local schools, coaching centres) operates under formal B2B conventions.

### Procurement Norms & Operational Requirements

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│         INSTITUTIONAL B2B PROCUREMENT CYCLE            │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Formal RFQ / WhatsApp Itemized Enquiry List        │
│ 2. Pro-forma Quotation with HSN Codes & GST Breakdown  │
│ 3. Purchase Order (PO) Issuance from School / Office   │
│ 4. Batch Delivery with Stamped Delivery Challan        │
│ 5. Tax Invoice (B2B GSTIN with e-Way Bill if > ₹50k)   │
│ 6. Credit Window: Payment in 15–30 Days (NEFT/RTGS)    │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### GST Rates & Invoicing Standards for Stationery (2025/2026 CBIC Classifications)

| Product Category | HSN Code | GST Rate | Invoicing Requirements |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **School Exercise Books, Notebooks, Registers** | **4820** | **0% (Nil)** / 12% | Standard GST invoice; exempt educational books show 0% tax column. |
| **Pencils, Crayons, Pastels, Chalks** | **9609** | **0% (Nil)** / 12% | Zero-rated educational category. |
| **Ballpoint, Gel, Fountain Pens & Refills** | **9608** | **18%** | 18% GST (9% CGST + 9% SGST). HSN 9608 must be explicitly itemized. |
| **Pencil Sharpeners & Erasers** | **8214 / 4016** | **12%** | 12% GST applicable. |
| **Geometry Boxes & Mathematical Instruments** | **9017** | **18%** | Standard 18% slab. |
| **Office Copier Paper (A4 / FS 75–80 GSM)** | **4802** | **12% / 18%** | Bulk reams for schools/offices. |
| **Filing Products (Solo Box Files, Cobra Files)** | **4820 / 3926** | **18%** | Office supply standard. |
| **Adhesives & Tapes (Fevicol, BoPP Packaging Tape)** | **3506 / 3919** | **18%** | Industrial & packaging standard. |

### Institutional Payment & Credit Terms
- **Credit Terms:** Established schools and offices expect **15 to 30 days credit** from delivery date. First-time buyers are often given **50% advance / 50% on delivery**.
- **Typical Order Value:**
  - Coaching Centres / Small Offices: ₹5,000 – ₹25,000 per month.
  - CBSE / State Board Schools (Term Start): ₹50,000 – ₹3,50,000 per bulk shipment (Copier paper, answer sheets, classroom registers, art exam paper).
- **Vendor Requirements:** Buyers demand valid GSTIN, state jurisdiction compliance (Kerala 32 code), bank NEFT/RTGS account details on invoice, and itemized HSN codes for input tax credit (ITC) reconciliation.
